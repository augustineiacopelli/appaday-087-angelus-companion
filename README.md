# appaday-087-angelus-companion
A guided Angelus and Regina Caeli. Pray along with the versicles, responses, and Hail Marys step by step or as full text, with a traditional 3-3-3-9 bell chime at 6 AM, noon, and 6 PM. Auto-switches to the Regina Caeli through Eastertide via the Computus. A quiet daily pause for the Incarnation.
